E3 satellite application: notes (generated by replication/scripts/e3_satellite.py)
================================================================================

DATA AND UNIT
  Input: replication/data/dhs/dhs_features.parquet (19,669 DHS clusters, 23 countries,
  survey years 2009-2016, 636 f_* feature columns in the file).
  Outcome y = wealth (cluster mean asset index, wealthpooled). Real-valued, may be negative.
  Unit of analysis = cluster. Every cluster has equal weight (unweighted).
  Feature count used: 636 f_* columns (constant histogram bins were removed in preparation).
  Country-years: grouped by (country, year). Groups with fewer than 50 clusters are dropped;
  dropped groups: none. Country-years analysed: 43.

CROSS-FITTING SCHEMES
  in_country : fold = fold_spatial (5 within-country k-means blocks). Holding out block k
               removes that block in every country; the pooled model is trained on the other
               four blocks across all countries.
  out_country: fold = fold_country (leave one country out, 23 folds).
  Every cluster receives its prediction from the fold that holds it out.

POINT MODELS
  ridge: StandardScaler fitted on the training fold, then clip to [-5, 5], then RidgeCV with
         alphas = logspace(-1, 3, 9) (generalised cross-validation on the training data).
  lgbm : LGBMRegressor, 600 trees, learning_rate 0.03, num_leaves 31, min_child_samples 40,
         subsample 0.8 (subsample_freq = 1 set, otherwise LightGBM ignores subsample),
         colsample_bytree 0.5, verbose -1, n_jobs 4. No feature standardisation (trees).

OUTER OOF AND CALIBRATION POOL (used by C1, C2, C4)
  Pass 1: for each point model, the OOF prediction of fold k is the model trained on all other
  folds (5 fits for in_country, 23 for out_country per model).
  Pass 2: for held-out fold k, the calibration, residual pool and decile edges use the OOF
  predictions and outcomes of the training clusters (fold != k).
  DEVIATION: the spec's main option for C1 (nested 3-fold inside each training fold) was not
  used. On the shared 4-core machine a single LightGBM fit took several seconds to minutes, so
  nested folds (about 4x the number of fits) would have exceeded the runtime goal. The spec's
  fallback is used for all of C1, C2 and C4.
  Leakage: the training clusters' OOF predictions in pass 2 come from models that were trained
  on fold k's outcomes. This can make the calibration and residual pools slightly optimistic
  for the held-out fold. The effect is expected to be small; it is not quantified here.

ESTIMATORS (all indices averaged over R = 200 draws where simulated)
  N : plug-in OOF prediction yhat.
  C1: y_cal = a + b * yhat. (a, b) by OLS of y on the OOF prediction over the training
      clusters of that outer fold. Applied to the held-out yhat.
      Median calibration slope b: in_country ridge 0.964, lgbm 0.982; out_country ridge 0.934, lgbm 0.974.
  C2: y* = yhat + e*, e* drawn with replacement from the pooled OOF residuals of the training
      clusters (y - yhat_oof). Homoskedastic.
  C3: quantile regression forest (quantile_forest.RandomForestQuantileRegressor, 300 trees,
      min_samples_leaf 20, max_features 0.3, n_jobs 4) fitted per outer fold on the training
      clusters. For each held-out cluster and each replicate, one of the 99 predicted
      quantiles (0.01, ..., 0.99) is drawn uniformly. C3 does not depend on the ridge/lgbm
      point model, so it is reported once with model label 'qrf'.
  C4: residual bootstrap within deciles of yhat. Deciles are the 10% quantiles of the OOF
      prediction over the training clusters. Each held-out cluster takes residuals
      from the training residuals in its decile bin, drawn with replacement.

INDICES (ineq.all_absolute, unweighted, translation invariant)
  abs_gini = half the mean absolute difference (ineq.abs_gini); sd; p90_p10 = weighted 0.9 and
  0.1 quantiles (ineq.wquantile, interpolated); share_below = share of clusters with y below
  a fixed cutoff = pooled 40th percentile of true wealth across all clusters = -0.3827
  (ineq.wquantile, not the np.quantile linear rule).

SUMMARY DEFINITIONS
  Relative bias (%) = mean over country-years of 100 * (estimate - truth) / truth.
  MAE share_below = mean over country-years of |estimate - truth| in percentage points.
  Spearman = rank correlation across country-years of estimated vs true abs_gini.
  share_lt_X = share of country-years in which the estimate is below the truth.
  lambda = Cov(yhat, y) / Var(y) within country-year, using the N prediction (population
  moments, ddof 0). OOF R^2 = 1 - SSE/SST within country-year, N prediction, the scheme's
  out-of-fold predictions. sd_ratio = sd(yhat) / sd(y).
  Proposition 4 note: mean-preserving linear shrinkage yhat = mu + lambda (y - mu) gives
  abs_gini(yhat)/abs_gini(y) = lambda exactly. When yhat is close to linear in y, the ratio
  abs_gini_N / abs_gini_true per country-year should be close to lambda. This is checked in
  e3_country_all.csv (ratio_N_over_true_abs_gini against lambda). The relation is approximate:
  yhat is not exactly linear in y, and the mean of yhat need not equal the mean of y.

OUTPUT FILES
  e3_results_raw.csv   one row per scheme x model x estimator x country-year, with true and
                       estimated abs_gini, sd, p90_p10, share_below.
  e3_summary.csv       per scheme x model x estimator summaries; median lambda, OOF R^2 and
                       sd ratio per scheme x point model (blank for qrf).
  e3_country.csv       lgbm in-country, per country-year: n, OOF R^2, lambda, true and
                       estimated abs_gini (N, C4).
  e3_country_all.csv   OOF R^2, lambda, sd ratio and N ratio for all scheme x point-model
                       combinations, per country-year.
  e3_table_main.tex    booktabs rows of the main table (no tabular environment).
  Fig_e3_country.pdf   in-country (a) and out-of-country (b), lgbm, N and C4.

RUNTIME (wall clock, shared 4-core machine; another heavy job ran concurrently)
  in_country: 867.7 s
  out_country: 7803.8 s
  total: 8679.1 s (144.7 min)

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
  - Survey weights and the unit of the DHS sampling design are not used (cluster unweighted).
  - Country-year sample sizes are small for some surveys (down to 140 clusters); estimates
    in those cells are noisier and affect the rank correlations.
  - Out-of-country predictions come from models trained on 22 countries.
  - C1, C2 and C4 use the outer OOF predictions of the training clusters (see the deviation
    above), which carries the mild leakage described there.
  - The C3 quantile forest is fitted once per fold and shared by both point models.
